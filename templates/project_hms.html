2. Exhaustive Role-Based Workspaces, Portals & AI Copilot

The platform enforces rigorous role boundaries across 4 core stakeholder portals and an embedded AI Assistant, each tailored with specialized workflows, interfaces, and contextual processors.

Super Admin

1. Platform Super Administrator Workspace

blueprints/admin
  • Doctor Registration & Verification Pipeline: Reviews pending doctor registrations with medical degree qualifications, state medical council registration numbers, specialization, clinic details, and address proof before activation.
  • Sequential Subscription Engine (FIFO): Immutable subscription periods with sequential start/end date queuing (DoctorSubscriptionPeriod), eliminating plan overwriting and preventing revenue leakage.
  • Emergency Recovery Identifier Governance: Generates a cryptographically randomized, unique 10-Digit Doctor Recovery Identifier upon approval. Governs doctor password recovery requests via verification audit trails.
  • Granular Subscription Packaging & Overrides: Dynamic creation and customization of subscription plans (STANDARD, PROFESSIONAL, ENTERPRISE) with doctor-level granular feature overrides.
  • Dedicated Doctor Profile Audit: Real-time operational KPI audit per clinic (Consultations, Patients, Prescriptions, Treatments, Staff, Subscription Revenue).
Doctor Workspace

2. Independent Doctor Workspace

blueprints/doctor
  • Clinical Dashboard & Operational Metrics: Real-time snapshot of today’s patient queue, completed consultations, expected wait times, active treatment courses, pending staff attendance, unread internal chat messages, and today's revenue.
  • Advanced Multi-Slot Scheduling Engine: Multi-session schedule builder (Morning & Evening clinic hours), customizable slot intervals (e.g. 15m, 20m, 30m), seat capacity limits per slot, custom date-range blocks, and holiday exceptions.
  • Sequential Live Queue Controller: Single-click queue progression (Call Next Patient, Mark With Doctor, Complete Consultation, Mark No-Show, Priority Overrides).
  • Digital Rx Generator with Smart Prefill: Fast prescription writer with structured medicine dosage grids, frequency, meal relation (Before/After Food), duration, diagnosis, and clinical advice with instant historical pre-fill.
  • Custom Prescription Letterhead Suite: Uploadable clinic header graphics, logos, footer banners, digital doctor signatures, and precise top/bottom print margins (in cm) for physical stationary printing.
  • Multi-Session Treatment Tracking & Staff Payroll: Multi-session treatment course logs, staff attendance rosters, and automated monthly salary calculations with deduction/bonus rules.

1. Concurrency Row-Locks

Enforces zero double-booking race conditions during simultaneous checkout requests via explicit transaction locking on booking_day_locks.

2. Real-Time IST Slot Pruning

For today's bookings, instantly prunes past hour slots according to Asia/Kolkata current time while allowing staff overrides for late arrivals.

3. Multi-Session Capacity

Supports Morning, Afternoon, and Evening schedule sessions with independent consultation intervals (10m, 15m, 20m, 30m, 45m, 60m).

⚙️ 02

Engine 2: Sequential Live Queue & Dynamic Wait Estimation

Deterministic token allocation, priority insertion, and live public waiting telemetry
services/queue.py
Sequential Daily Token Allocation

Upon patient arrival check-in on the appointment date, the system issues an incremental, sequential queue token (Token #1, Token #2, etc.) isolated per doctor and date.

Dynamic Real-Time Wait Calculation

Live wait calculation: Estimated Wait = (Tokens Ahead of Patient) × (Doctor Session Slot Duration). Updates instantly when consultations conclude.

Engine 3: Digital Prescription & Custom Letterhead Designer

Dosage grids, smart prefill, margin calibration, and ReportLab vector PDF export
services/prescription_export.py
1. Structured Medicine Grids

Medicine name, dosage, frequency (1-0-1), food relation (Before/After Food), duration in days, diagnosis, and advice notes.

2. Smart Prefill Intelligence

Automatically fetches prior clinical notes, chronic medications, and dosage histories for returning patients to save doctor typing time.

3. Print Margin Calibration

Configurable top and bottom print margins (in cm) allowing printing directly onto physical clinic letterhead stationery or blank paper.

Session progress tracking, procedure prep directives, and follow-up fee waivers

Configurable treatment items with pricing, duration, prep times, and staff directives.

2. Multi-Session Course Tracking

For procedures requiring multiple sittings (Orthodontics, Root Canals, PRP, Physiotherapy): total planned vs completed sessions counter (e.g. Session 3 of 6), date logs, and clinical observation notes.

3. Intelligent Fee & Waiver Calculator

Automatically computes consultation fees, procedure pricing, and repeat visit waivers (e.g. Free consultation if visiting within doctor-configured window).

  • Drug Master Catalog: Medicine name, generic name, category (Tablet, Syrup, Injection, Ointment), manufacturer, batch number, expiry date, purchase price, and selling price.
  • Real-Time Stock Deductions: Automatic quantity deduction from inventory upon prescription dispensing.
  • Low-Stock Alerting: Real-time highlight of medicines reaching or falling below configured reorder threshold levels.
⚙️ 08

Engine 8: Razorpay Payments & Indian GST Invoicing

services/payment.py & services/billing.py
  • Razorpay Online Payments: Integrated payment gateway for booking advance fees and SaaS subscriptions.
  • Tamper-Proof Webhook Processing: Asynchronous HMAC-SHA256 signature verification endpoint (/api/v1/razorpay/webhook) ensuring zero payment drops.
  • Indian GST Billing Engine: Automatic calculation of Taxable Base, CGST, SGST, and IGST with vector PDF invoice generation via ReportLab.
  • Multi-Method Tracking: Supports ONLINE_RAZORPAY, CASH, UPI, CARD, and NETBANKING.
⚙️ 09

Engine 9: JhaMed AI Assistant & Role-Aware Copilot

services/chatbot/
  • 20 Controlled Domain Tools: AI agent executes authorized backend operations (appointment lookups, queue status, staff permission inspection, Rx lookup).
  • Strict RBAC & Tenant Isolation: Every AI query enforces doctor/clinic boundary checks and staff permission gating before tool execution.
  • Ephemeral In-Memory Privacy: Zero permanent database conversational retention with 15-minute inactivity auto-expiry protecting clinical privacy.
  • Prompt Injection Defense: System prompt hardening and strict output sanitization preventing hallucinated actions.
⚙️ 10

Engine 10: Centralized India Timezone Architecture

services/time_service.py
  • ZoneInfo Centralization: Standardized on ZoneInfo("Asia/Kolkata") (IST, UTC+5:30) across all business workflows.
  • Storage vs Presentation Fidelity: PostgreSQL timestamp columns store clean, naive UTC (TIMESTAMP WITHOUT TIME ZONE).
  • Business Calculations: Today's clinic queue, doctor metrics, slot boundaries, and staff attendance strictly evaluate get_ist_today() and get_ist_time().
  • Jinja Skew Prevention: UI templates render stored timestamps using |ist_datetime and |ist_date Jinja filters with zero timezone skew across midnight/day boundaries.
