CareFlow HMS — Enterprise Healthcare SaaS

A doctor-first, multi-tenant Hospital & Clinic Management SaaS platform engineered with Flask 3, SQLAlchemy ORM, PostgreSQL, Resend REST API, and ZoneInfo IST.

Visit Live App (hms.codehypetech.in) Technical Deep-Dive →
Multi-Tenant Isolation

Doctor-First Isolated Practice Environments

CareFlow HMS delivers an end-to-end digital practice ecosystem for independent healthcare practitioners and clinics. Every doctor receives a dedicated, high-speed public clinic webpage (/dr/doctor-slug) with customized branding, treatment catalogs, and real-time appointment booking.

Isolated Multi-Tenancy

Independent queues, staff rosters, patient EHRs, and letterhead configurations.

Dedicated Public Microsite

Instant /dr/slug portals with booking filters and live seat availability.

ZoneInfo IST Standard

Asia/Kolkata timezone standard with naive UTC PostgreSQL storage.

Resend REST API Transport

Non-blocking HTTPS transactional email for receipts, invites, and notifications.

01
Slot Concurrency Row-Lock

Prevents overbooking race conditions via booking_day_locks table with SELECT FOR UPDATE.

02
Sequential Live Queue

Calculates dynamic token wait: Wait = (Tokens Ahead) × (Avg Slot Duration).

03
Digital Rx & Letterhead

Dosage grid, smart prefill from patient history, cm margin print alignment, and QR token verification.

04
Multi-Session Procedures

Session progress logs (e.g. Session 3 of 6), repeat-patient fee waiver calculator (free follow-up within 30 days).

05
Staff Attendance & Payroll

Automated salary calculator: Base monthly pay, per-day deductions for leaves/half-days, bonuses, and payment logs.

06
24h Auto-Expiry Chat

Direct clinic channel with patient token tagging, urgent directives, role broadcasts, and 24h auto-wipe.
