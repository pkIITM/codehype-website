SYSTEM WORKFLOW

End-to-End Operational Pipeline

The platform ensures secure user onboarding, automated welcome email delivery, and multi-tier access separation between trekkers, guides, and platform administrators.

  • User Registration: Customer accounts onboarded with form validation and password hashing.
  • Automated Notifications: Flask-Mail SMTP dispatches instant confirmation emails to registered users.
  • Staff Approval Pipeline: Guide & staff accounts require SuperAdmin vetting before dashboard access is granted.
  • Expedition Records: Centralized database storing trip manifests, participant medical details, and guide rosters.
